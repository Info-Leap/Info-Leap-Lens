# Respondent: Priya Sharma | Mumbai | Age 32 | Salaried Professional | Segment: Safety-First Investor

## Background
Priya works as a marketing manager at an FMCG company. She has been investing for 4 years. Currently holds FDs, mutual funds (SIP), and some physical gold jewellery. No crypto exposure.

## Opening — Current Investment Behaviour
**Moderator:** Tell me about how you currently manage your savings and investments.

**Priya:** So I have my salary coming in every month, and I do a SIP in a large-cap fund — about 8,000 a month. Then I have FDs for my emergency fund. Gold I have in the form of jewellery that my parents gave me, but I don't think of that as investment really. It's just... there.

**Moderator:** Why do you hold FDs along with mutual funds?

**Priya:** Because FDs are safe. I know exactly what I'm getting. With mutual funds I've seen my portfolio go down and it's stressful. So I like having something stable.

## Digital Gold Awareness
**Moderator:** Have you heard of digital gold?

**Priya:** Yes, I've seen ads. I think it's like... you buy gold but it's stored somewhere for you? I downloaded Paytm once and I think it showed me that option. But I never bought it.

**Moderator:** What stopped you?

**Priya:** Honestly, I wasn't sure who is holding it. Like if Paytm has my gold, and Paytm shuts down tomorrow, what happens? I don't trust it fully.

## Concept Exposure — CoinDCX Digital Gold with 5-7% Yield
**Moderator:** [Shows concept] CoinDCX is offering a digital gold product where your gold earns 5-7% yield annually. The gold is lent out to jewellers who pay this interest.

**Priya:** Oh, interesting. So it's like a gold FD? That's actually a nice idea.

**Moderator:** What's your first reaction?

**Priya:** My first reaction is — this sounds too good. Gold already protects against inflation, and now also giving 7%? I'd want to know: is my gold safe? What if the jeweller doesn't return it?

**Moderator:** The gold is backed by SEBI-regulated custodians.

**Priya:** SEBI I trust more than a fintech company name I don't know. But CoinDCX is a crypto company, right? I don't do crypto. Will my gold be used for crypto things?

## Trust and Barriers
**Moderator:** What would make you feel comfortable trying this?

**Priya:** If a bank was involved. Like if HDFC said this is safe, I'd consider it. Or if there's an insurance on my gold amount. Right now I'd start with maybe 5,000 rupees just to test. Not more.

**Moderator:** What's your concern about the yield?

**Priya:** 7% sounds like mutual fund returns. But gold is supposed to be safe, not risky. If I'm getting 7% it means someone is taking a risk. Who is taking that risk? Me or CoinDCX?

## Preferred Route
**Moderator:** [Shows Route 1: Safety-led messaging] vs [Route 2: Returns-led messaging]

**Priya:** Route 1 for sure. "Your gold, always yours, earning for you." That resonates. I don't care so much about returns as I care about safety. Route 2 sounds like a mutual fund ad.

## Final Advice
**Priya:** I think this is a good product for people like me who have idle gold at home. But you need to fix the trust issue with CoinDCX's crypto image. Partner with a bank or show a government backing. And start with smaller investment amounts so people can try.
