# Respondent: Aditya Nair | Bangalore | Age 35 | Software Engineer | Segment: Yield-Seeker

## Background
Aditya works at a mid-size tech company. Heavy investor — equity, mutual funds, some US stocks via Vested. Has used gold ETFs. Analytical decision-maker. High financial literacy.

## Opening — Current Investment Behaviour
**Moderator:** Walk me through your investment portfolio.

**Aditya:** Sure. 60% equity — mix of direct stocks and mutual funds. 20% in a US ETF portfolio via Vested. 10% debt — short-duration funds. 10% gold ETFs. Gold is mostly for portfolio balance, not because I love gold.

**Moderator:** Why gold ETFs over physical gold?

**Aditya:** Purity, liquidity, no storage cost, no making charges. Gold ETF tracks LBMA price directly. Physical gold has 5-10% loss on resale. It's just better.

## Digital Gold vs Gold ETF Comparison
**Moderator:** Have you looked at digital gold products?

**Aditya:** I've seen them but I prefer ETFs. Digital gold has a spread — buy/sell price difference — and some platforms add storage charges. ETFs are cleaner.

**Moderator:** What would make digital gold attractive over an ETF?

**Aditya:** Yield. ETFs give zero yield. If digital gold gave 5-7% on top of price appreciation, the math suddenly changes. That would outperform most gold ETFs significantly.

## Concept Exposure — CoinDCX Digital Gold with 5-7% Yield
**Moderator:** [Shows concept]

**Aditya:** Okay so this is like a gold lending program. Interesting. Let me think through this. Gold price appreciation — say 8% historically. Plus 5-7% yield. That's 13-15% total return. Minus any storage or platform fee. That's competitive with equity for a "safe" asset. I'd look at this seriously.

**Moderator:** What questions do you have?

**Aditya:** Key questions: 1) What's the counterparty risk if the jeweller defaults? 2) Is there insurance on the lent gold? 3) Is the yield fixed or variable? 4) What's the tax treatment — is yield taxed as interest income? 5) What's the exit — can I sell anytime at spot price?

## CoinDCX Brand Association
**Moderator:** Reaction to the CoinDCX name?

**Aditya:** Mixed. I know them as a crypto exchange — solid tech company, regulatory compliant. But expanding to gold is different business model. Do they have expertise in gold custody and jeweller relationship management? That's not a crypto skill.

**Moderator:** They're partnering with established gold custodians.

**Aditya:** Then show me who the custodian is on the product page. Prominently. MMTC-PAMP or Brinks — those names are credible. CoinDCX's brand alone doesn't close the trust gap for me.

## Benchmark Comparison
**Moderator:** How does this compare to Sovereign Gold Bonds?

**Aditya:** SGB gives 2.5% fixed yield, gold appreciation, and tax-free gains after 8 years. That's government guaranteed. CoinDCX gives higher yield (5-7%) but with: variable rate risk, counterparty risk, and presumably taxable gains. I'd take CoinDCX if the yield floor is at least 4% guaranteed and there's liquidity. The SGB 8-year lock-in is painful.

## Preferred Route
**Moderator:** Route 1 vs Route 2?

**Aditya:** Route 2 for me — the returns framing. But I'd want the fine print visible. "5-7%" needs to say "variable, based on jeweller demand" not just a number. Transparency matters more than the number itself.

## Final Advice
**Aditya:** Strong product concept. The yield differentiation is real and meaningful. Execute on: full fee transparency, custodian disclosure, minimum guaranteed floor yield, and SEBI-compliant documentation. If you do those things, you'll capture the financially literate segment easily. We want to be convinced, not sold to.
