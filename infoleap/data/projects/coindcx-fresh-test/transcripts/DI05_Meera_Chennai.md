# Respondent: Meera Krishnan | Chennai | Age 38 | Teacher | Segment: Safety-First Investor

## Background
Meera teaches at a private school. Husband is a government employee. Conservative investor. PPF, NPS, postal savings. Has physical gold from family. No stocks or mutual funds — finds them "too risky."

## Opening — Current Investment Behaviour
**Moderator:** How do you save and invest?

**Meera:** We are very conservative. PPF we both contribute to maximum — 1.5 lakhs each. NPS for my husband's retirement. Some RDs. And gold — we have a lot of gold from my side of the family. South Indian families, you know, gold is everything.

**Moderator:** How much gold approximately?

**Meera:** Oh, at least 300-400 grams in jewellery. Plus my husband bought some gold coins for our daughter's future.

**Moderator:** Do you ever sell your gold?

**Meera:** Never. Gold is kept for important occasions — daughter's wedding, emergencies. We don't sell it.

## Concept of Making Gold Work
**Moderator:** What if your idle gold could earn something?

**Meera:** That sounds good but I'm suspicious. How? Gold earning money sounds like those chit fund scams. I've heard of people losing money.

**Moderator:** [Explains digital gold lending concept]

**Meera:** Lending my gold to jewellers? But what if jeweller doesn't return? In south India many people have lost gold this way — given to a jeweller for work and he runs away. This is a trauma for many families.

**Moderator:** This is regulated — jeweller provides collateral.

**Meera:** What collateral? I want to see this clearly. Is it written down? Is it legally binding?

## Concept Exposure — CoinDCX Digital Gold with 5-7% Yield
**Moderator:** [Shows full concept including regulation, SEBI backing]

**Meera:** 5-7% is attractive. That's like PPF returns but on gold which also grows in value. But my concern is: this is a mobile app company? Not a bank? Not post office?

**Moderator:** It's a regulated financial technology company.

**Meera:** I don't use fintech for big amounts. I use SBI, post office. These I trust because they've been there for 50 years. How long has CoinDCX been there?

**Moderator:** About 5-6 years, and they've handled crores in crypto transactions.

**Meera:** Crypto I don't understand and I don't trust. This is a crypto company?

## Yield Comprehension
**Moderator:** Do you understand what 5-7% per year means on gold?

**Meera:** So if I have 100 grams of gold, I'd get 5-7 grams more each year? Or in money?

**Moderator:** In money — equivalent to 5-7% of the gold's value.

**Meera:** Oh, in money. Not in gold. So gold price also keeps changing, and I'm getting money on top?

**Moderator:** Yes.

**Meera:** That's complicated. I prefer simple products. PPF — I put in money, I know what I get. This has too many moving parts.

## Preferred Route
**Moderator:** Route 1 vs Route 2?

**Meera:** Route 1 — definitely. "Always yours, always safe." Safety is everything for me. Returns come second.

## Ideal Product
**Moderator:** What would your ideal version of this product look like?

**Meera:** Post office or SBI should offer this. Same concept but through a channel I trust. Or if my bank sends me an SMS about this, I might enquire. App-only products I don't trust for large amounts.

## Final Advice
**Meera:** Good idea but wrong company for people like me. Either get a bank to co-brand this, or get a credible endorsement from somewhere. And simplify the explanation — "your gold earns 5-7% per year, like interest on an FD but on your gold" — one line, simple, clear. That I can explain to my husband.
